# Terminology audit — candidate list

Generated from the existing content on 20 September 2026. Every proper noun
below becomes a **variable** during migration, so confirming it once fixes it
everywhere.

## How to use this

Tick a term when you've confirmed it is current. Strike it out and write the
replacement when it isn't. Terms are ordered by how badly a stale one hurts.

Counts are `flowcharts / FAQ`. Where the two sources disagree, **the FAQ wins.**

### Two different reasons a term is flowchart-only

An early version of this list flagged every flowchart-only term as a staleness
risk. That was wrong, and the distinction matters:

- **🔒 internal by design** — the term *should* only appear in internal
  content. Eli, TechOne and ECM are staff tools; the FAQ is public, so their
  absence there is correct, not a warning. These carry no extra risk.
- **⚠️ unreviewed** — the term could reasonably have appeared in public content
  but didn't, so it has had no recent eyes on it. These are the real risk.

## Method

Proper-noun candidates were extracted from the flowchart node bodies and
context articles in `CustomerService.html`, and from every question, answer,
intro, footer, stepper and variable value in `FAQ.json`. HTML tags, URLs and
emoji were stripped first. 374 raw candidates were produced; obvious sentence
fragments and generic phrases were removed by hand to leave the list above.

The extraction is reproducible, so it can be re-run after migration to catch
anything introduced later.

## What happens to these

Each confirmed term becomes a variable with:

- an `ownerId` — the department that can answer for it
- a verification `question` — generated, then sent to that department
- a `type` — `system`, `form`, `org`, `text`
- `internal: true` where it must never reach public FAQ copy (most of §1 and §3)

At which point this audit stops being a one-off and becomes the standing
staleness report described in the spec.
